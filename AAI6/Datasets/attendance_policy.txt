UNIVERSITY ATTENDANCE POLICY

Students are expected to attend all scheduled lectures, tutorials and practical sessions.
The minimum attendance requirement for appearing in the End Semester Examination (ESE) is 75%.
Attendance is calculated separately for each registered course.
Students having attendance below 75% may not automatically be considered eligible for the End Semester Examination.
Attendance records are maintained by the respective course instructors.
Students are responsible for periodically checking their attendance records.
Special cases involving medical or other approved reasons are governed by the separate Medical Leave and Attendance Condonation Policy.

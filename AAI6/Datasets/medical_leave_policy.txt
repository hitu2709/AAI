MEDICAL LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE CONDONATION POLICY

A student who is unable to attend classes because of illness may submit an application for medical leave.
The application must be supported by appropriate medical documents.
Approval of medical leave does not automatically guarantee eligibility for the End Semester Examination.
Students whose attendance falls below the normal attendance requirement may apply for attendance condonation.
The condonation request is reviewed by the appropriate academic authority.
The final examination eligibility of such students depends on the approved condonation decision and the applicable examination regulations.
Students should therefore verify their final eligibility status before appearing for the examination.

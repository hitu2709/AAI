MAJOR PROJECT GUIDELINES

The Major Project is carried out under the supervision of an assigned faculty guide.
Students are expected to identify a suitable problem, conduct a literature review, design the proposed solution, implement the system and evaluate the results.

The project report should include:
1. Introduction
2. Literature Review
3. Problem Definition
4. Proposed Methodology
5. Implementation
6. Results and Evaluation
7. Conclusion
8. References

Students must periodically present their progress to the project guide.
The final evaluation consists of project demonstration, report evaluation, presentation and viva voce.

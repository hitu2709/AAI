INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES

Students are encouraged to undertake internships that provide exposure to professional engineering environments.
An internship may be completed in industry, research organizations, startups or approved academic institutions.
Students must maintain records of the work completed during the internship.
At the end of the internship, students must submit an internship report.
The report should contain the organization profile, objectives, work performed, technologies used, learning outcomes and conclusion.
The internship evaluation may include report assessment, presentation and viva voce.
Students must follow the submission schedule communicated by the department.

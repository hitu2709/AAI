END SEMESTER EXAMINATION POLICY

Students must satisfy the academic requirements applicable to their registered courses before appearing for the End Semester Examination.
The normal minimum attendance requirement is 75%.
Students with attendance shortages are governed by the applicable attendance condonation rules.
An approved medical leave application by itself does not constitute final examination eligibility.
Where attendance condonation is required, examination eligibility is established only after the relevant condonation decision has been approved.
Students must also complete examination registration within the announced deadline.
Failure to complete examination registration may prevent a student from appearing for the examination even if other academic requirements are satisfied.
